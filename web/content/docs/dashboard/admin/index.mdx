the admin panel holds two kinds of page: site-scoped pages, for the people who run a site, and internal pages, for owlette itself.

---

## site-scoped pages

these open for anyone who is an **owner** or **admin** of the whole site on at least one site. the sidebar groups them: **fleet** holds sites and agent tokens, **swoop** holds live sessions and settings, and **configuration** holds schedules, alerts and webhooks.

| page | URL | what it does |
|------|-----|--------------|
| sites | `/admin/sites` | list your sites, and open one's settings: its name and timezone, its members with their roles, swoop modes and access, its machines and keep screens awake, and deleting it. see [sites](/docs/dashboard/admin/sites) and [member management](/docs/dashboard/admin/member-management) |
| agent tokens | `/admin/tokens` | list and revoke a site's agent refresh tokens. see [token management](/docs/dashboard/admin/token-management) |
| swoop | `/admin/swoop` | turn [swoop](/docs/dashboard/swoop#turning-it-on) on or off for each site, see everyone in a swoop session right now, and end a session |
| hoot | `/admin/hoot` | a site's [hoot](/docs/dashboard/hoot) approval setting and the machines that may act on others through [hoot mcp](/docs/agent/hoot-mcp) |
| schedules | `/admin/schedules` | manage [schedule presets](/docs/dashboard/admin/schedule-presets) |
| alerts | `/admin/alerts` | manage a site's threshold alert rules. see [email alerts](/docs/dashboard/admin/email-alerts) |
| webhooks | `/admin/webhooks` | manage a site's outbound [webhook](/docs/dashboard/admin/webhooks) subscriptions |

**sites** lists every site you belong to, with a gear on each one you own or administer with whole-site access; a site where your access is [limited to some machines](/docs/dashboard/admin/member-management#machine-scope) reads, for example, **admin · 2 machines**, with no gear. the other pages act on one site at a time: their site selector offers the sites you belong to, and a change goes through only on a site where you are owner or admin of the whole site; on a site where you are a plain member, or limited to some machines, the server refuses it. the swoop pages leave those sites out: neither live sessions nor settings lists them.

---

## internal pages

installers (`/admin/installers`), the template library (`/admin/presets`), users (`/admin/users`) and email (`/admin/email`) cover platform-wide operations run by owlette. they are hidden from the sidebar for customer accounts, and their routes send anyone else back to the dashboard.

---

## roles

your role is set **per site**: the same person can own one site, administer a second and be a plain member of a third. the role on your owlette account grants nothing on a site by itself. the one exception is owlette's superadmins, who reach every site.

| role on a site | what it can do there | admin panel |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **member** | read-only: view machines, capture screenshots, open live view, and watch a [swoop](/docs/dashboard/swoop) session when the site lets members watch | none from this site |
| **admin** | everything a member can, plus machine commands and configuration, swoop control and swoop settings, deployments, roosts, talons, webhooks, logs, alert rules, enrolling machines, revoking their tokens and managing members | the seven site-scoped pages above, with whole-site access |
| **owner** | everything an admin can, plus deleting the site and transferring ownership. every site has exactly one | the seven site-scoped pages above |

a person's [swoop mode](/docs/dashboard/admin/member-management#swoop-mode) on a site can change what they get from swoop there, and nothing else.

a member or an admin can also be [limited to some machines](/docs/dashboard/admin/member-management#machine-scope) of a site. on those machines they keep their role and their swoop mode; everything that spans the site, from the admin panel to deployments, roost and talons, needs the whole site. the owner always has the whole site.

---

## getting admin on a site

ask an owner or admin of that site to change your role. the change applies to that site only, and it takes effect at once. they make it, with any change to your swoop mode, in the site's members section: see [changing a role](/docs/dashboard/admin/member-management#changing-a-role).

ownership is not a role anyone can assign: it moves by transfer, from the current owner (or an owlette superadmin).

---

## opening the panel

- **profile menu** → **admin panel**. the entry shows once you are an owner or admin of the whole site on at least one site, and opens on **sites** (owlette's superadmins land on installers)
- **direct URLs**: `/admin/sites`, `/admin/sites/{siteId}`, `/admin/tokens`, `/admin/swoop`, `/admin/hoot`, `/admin/schedules`, `/admin/alerts`, `/admin/webhooks`

someone who is owner or admin of the whole site on no site, an admin limited to some machines included, is sent back to the dashboard with **access denied**, and so is a site admin who opens an internal page directly.

---

## capability matrix

every permission check in the web app asks for one capability. a site-scoped capability is held only on the site where you have that role; reading the sites you belong to is baseline membership, not a capability.

| capability | member | admin | owner | scope |
|-----------|:------:|:-----:|:-----:|-------|
| read access to the site | yes | yes | yes | site membership |
| `MACHINE_VIEW` (screenshots, live view) | yes | yes | yes | site, or your machines |
| `MACHINE_REMOTE_VIEW` (watch swoop) | yes | yes | yes | site, or your machines |
| `MACHINE_EXEC_COMMAND` | no | yes | yes | site, or your machines |
| `MACHINE_REMOTE_CONTROL` (control swoop) | no | yes | yes | site, or your machines |
| `SWOOP_SETTINGS_MANAGE` | no | yes | yes | site |
| `FLEET_SETTINGS_MANAGE` ([hoot mcp](/docs/agent/hoot-mcp): which machines may act on others) | no | yes | yes | site |
| `MACHINE_CONFIG_WRITE` | no | yes | yes | site, or your machines |
| `MACHINE_REMOVE` | no | yes | yes | site |
| `MACHINE_ENROLL` (installers, setup tokens, pairing codes) | no | yes | yes | site |
| `AGENT_TOKEN_REVOKE` | no | yes | yes | site |
| `DEPLOYMENT_MANAGE` | no | yes | yes | site |
| `DISTRIBUTION_MANAGE` (roost) | no | yes | yes | site |
| `UNINSTALL_TRIGGER` | no | yes | yes | site |
| `PRESET_MANAGE` (site presets) | no | yes | yes | site |
| `SITE_MEMBER_MANAGE` | no | yes | yes | site |
| `WEBHOOK_MANAGE` | no | yes | yes | site |
| `SITE_LOGS_MANAGE` | no | yes | yes | site |
| `TALON_MANAGE` | no | yes | yes | site |
| `ALERT_RULES_MANAGE` | no | yes | yes | site |
| `SITE_DELETE` | no | no | yes | site |
| `USER_SELF_PREFS` | yes | yes | yes | your own account |
| `USER_SELF_DELETE` | yes | yes | yes | your own account |
| `USER_ROLE_MANAGE` | no | no | no | platform |
| `USER_DELETE` | no | no | no | platform |
| `SYSTEM_PRESET_MANAGE` (deployment template presets) | no | no | no | platform |
| `INSTALLER_MANAGE` | no | no | no | platform |
| `ISSUE_MANAGE` (issue triage status) | no | no | no | platform |
| `GLOBAL_SETTINGS_WRITE` | no | no | no | platform |

the six platform capabilities back the internal pages, and no site role holds them. a superadmin holds every capability on every site.

**your machines**: someone [limited to some machines](/docs/dashboard/admin/member-management#machine-scope) of a site holds the five capabilities marked so only on those machines, at their role, and holds no other capability of that site, whatever their role. a machine outside their access answers `404`, the same answer whether it exists or not; anything that spans the site answers `403`. everyone else holds them on the whole site.

a capability is the floor, not the whole check. watching swoop also needs the site's `membersMayWatch` setting for a member with no swoop mode of their own, and taking control also needs a fresh second factor; see [watching versus control](/docs/dashboard/swoop#watching-versus-control). a swoop mode on a person's member row overrides their role for `MACHINE_REMOTE_VIEW` and `MACHINE_REMOTE_CONTROL` only, and never on the owner's row; see [swoop mode](/docs/dashboard/admin/member-management#swoop-mode).

hoot follows the same line. anyone who can read a site can chat with hoot about it, but a member's hoot gets read-only tools; an owner's or admin's hoot can also run commands, change configuration and deploy. limited to some machines, hoot works on those machines only, and offers none of the tools that span the site, deploying included; see [hoot](/docs/dashboard/hoot#choosing-machines). LLM keys are per user, managed by each person in their own [account settings](/docs/dashboard/account-settings#hoot); there is no shared key for an admin to manage.
