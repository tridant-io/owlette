**location**: admin panel → sites (`/admin/sites`)

<Callout type="info" title={"permissions"}>

the page opens for anyone who is an owner or admin on at least one site, and lists every site they belong to. owlette's superadmins see every site. anyone else is sent back to the dashboard with **access denied**.

</Callout>

the site switcher's **manage sites** opens this page. its **site settings** opens the [settings](#site-settings) of the site you are on, and is offered only where you are owner or admin.

---

## the site list

| column | what it shows |
|---|---|
| **name** | the site's name. a `current` badge marks the site you are on, which comes first; the rest follow by name |
| **site ID** | the site's permanent ID. click it to copy it |
| **timezone** | the site's timezone, or `UTC` when it has none |
| **role** | your role on the site: `owner`, `admin` or `member`. owlette's superadmins see the site's owner here instead: the owner's email, `you` on their own sites, or `—` when none is recorded |
| gear | opens the site's settings. it is there only on sites where you are owner or admin, and on every site for owlette's superadmins |

on a phone, a row puts the name and the gear on its first line, and the ID, timezone and role on its second.

with two or more sites, **filter sites…** narrows the list to the sites whose name, ID or timezone contains what you type (and, for owlette's superadmins, whose owner's email does). **Escape** clears it. while it is on, the list says how many of your sites it shows.

a site you only belong to as a member has no gear. open its settings anyway and the page says you're a member, and to ask its owner or an admin.

## new site

three places make a site:

- **new site**, at the top of this page. the new site's settings open, and the dashboard stays on the site you were on
- the site switcher's **new site**, offered only while you own or administer no site. you switch to the new site, and it becomes the site you come back to
- **create your first site**, on an empty dashboard, when you belong to no site at all

each one opens **create new site**: enter a **site name**, keep the generated site ID or set one under **customize site ID**, check the **site timezone** (read from your browser; **change timezone** picks another), and click **create site**. you own the new site. its ID is permanent and appears in URLs.

## site settings

`/admin/sites/{siteId}` holds one site's settings. open it from the site's gear, or from the site switcher's **site settings** for the site you are on. it shows the site's name, its ID (click it to copy) and your role, then one section at a time: **general**, **members**, **machines** and, for the site's owner, **danger zone**.

the section is part of the URL, `?section=members`, `machines` or `danger` (general needs none), so a link can open one and a reload keeps it. an unknown section, or one you can't see, opens **general**. a site you can't reach reads **site not found**. an older link to `?section=swoop` opens swoop settings.

### general

the **site name**, and the **timezone** that its talons and site-time schedules run on. a change applies when you click **save**, and the name can't be empty.

### members

the people who can reach the site, with their site role and swoop mode. adding, removing and changing them is in [member management](/docs/dashboard/admin/member-management).

### machines

first, the **keep screens awake** switch, **on** by default: machines on this site never sleep, blank or lock. it acts the moment it moves; there is no save. see [keep screens awake](/docs/dashboard/getting-started#keep-screens-awake).

then each of the site's machines, with whether it is online or when it was last seen, its agent version (not on a phone), and **awake** while it holds keep screens awake. each row has two buttons:

- **restart** works while the machine is online. it asks you to confirm, then restarts the machine; inside the machine's active hours it asks first, as the dashboard does: see [inside active hours](/docs/dashboard/machine-monitoring#inside-active-hours)
- **remove** takes the machine off the site for good, after a confirm that lists what goes with it

add a machine from the dashboard's **+** button.

### danger zone

only the site's owner sees it, and owlette's superadmins. **delete site** asks once more, then deletes the site for good:

- its machines and their history, its roosts, talons and webhooks, and everyone's access to it are removed
- its machines' agent tokens are revoked, so they stop reporting until someone pairs them to another site
- its site ID is retired, and can't be used again

the dialog stays open until the delete finishes, and says why if it fails. afterwards you land on **sites**, or on the dashboard when you no longer own or administer any site.
